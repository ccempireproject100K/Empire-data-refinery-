# UNVERIFIED Skills — Read This First

**Everything in this directory is UNVERIFIED.**

These are candidate skill entries that the archive's editors could **not**
verify to the standard the verified archive requires: the specific source
(a video, course, or companion repository) could not be located and checked
directly, or a license permitting a derived summary could not be established.

Rules for this section:

- Entries here are **not** part of the verified AI Skills Archive.
- They are **excluded** from the verified skill count (63 verified skills as
  of the 2026-10-17 harvest round 3) and from `site/public/skills.json`.
- They are indexed separately in `site/public/skills-unverified.json`.
- Each entry states **why** it could not be verified.
- An entry is promoted into the verified archive only after its source has
  been located, opened, and checked, and its license position established —
  at which point it is rewritten as a derived summary in the editors' own
  words, like every verified skill.
- When in doubt, material goes here, not into the verified archive. Nothing
  in this section is silently dropped, and nothing here is blended into the
  verified counts.
